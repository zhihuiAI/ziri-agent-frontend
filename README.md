# Ziri

**Enterprise AI Agent Platform for governed, end-to-end business execution**

[简体中文](./README.zh-CN.md) | English

Ziri connects enterprise knowledge, data, models, tools, and business systems so AI can move beyond answering questions and complete real work within defined organizational boundaries.

Starting from a business goal, Ziri understands enterprise context, plans the work, invokes the required systems, introduces human review for critical actions, and produces results that can be verified and traced.

[Try Ziri](https://ziri.aisky.online)

## Why Ziri

General-purpose agents and workflow tools are good at model invocation, tool connectivity, and task orchestration. Enterprise execution requires more: shared business definitions, current knowledge, inherited permissions, approval controls, and evidence that a task was completed correctly.

Ziri provides a governed execution layer between business tasks and the underlying AI capabilities. It helps organizations turn fragmented tools into a reusable enterprise AI system.

## Core Capabilities

### Goal-driven execution

Describe the outcome you need. Ziri breaks the goal into steps, identifies the required knowledge and systems, executes the plan, and checks the result against the completion criteria.

### Enterprise context

Combine identity, organizational roles, business entities, metric definitions, knowledge versions, data relationships, and permission boundaries into a consistent context for every task.

### Knowledge and system connectivity

Connect enterprise knowledge, data, tools, and systems such as ERP, CRM, OA/DMS, and MES through workflows, APIs, and compatible integration gateways.

### Trusted execution

Preserve sources, knowledge versions, system calls, validation results, approvals, and outputs throughout the task. High-risk actions can require confirmation or approval instead of being executed automatically.

### Business Agents and digital workers

Configure reusable Agents for specific roles, processes, and industry scenarios. Turn proven task methods into repeatable digital workflows that teams can safely share.

### Enterprise AI governance

Manage access to people, knowledge, data, tools, models, and Agents. Track execution quality, human intervention, cost, efficiency, and business outcomes from a unified control layer.

### Flexible deployment and model access

Support SaaS, dedicated environments, and private deployment according to enterprise infrastructure and security requirements. Public, private, and locally deployed models can be connected based on the selected solution.

## How It Works

1. **Understand the task** — identify the business goal, objects, rules, and completion criteria.
2. **Build a plan** — break down the work and determine the required data, knowledge, tools, and systems.
3. **Execute across systems** — retrieve information, invoke tools, perform actions, and record the process.
4. **Apply human control** — route sensitive or high-risk actions to confirmation and approval.
5. **Verify and deliver** — validate the business result and produce a reviewable deliverable with a traceable execution record.

## Typical Use Cases

- **Finance:** bank reconciliation, payment matching, expense review, management analysis, and month-end checks.
- **Sales:** account research, meeting preparation, opportunity follow-up, and CRM updates.
- **Quality and compliance:** SOP review, regulatory comparison, risk identification, and remediation tracking.
- **Research and knowledge work:** literature research, technical analysis, project knowledge organization, and report generation.
- **Human resources:** résumé screening, interview preparation, talent review, and training material preparation.
- **Operations:** data consolidation, workflow reminders, cross-functional coordination, and operating reports.

## Platform Architecture

Ziri organizes enterprise AI into five connected layers:

1. **Unified access:** Web, desktop, and enterprise collaboration platforms.
2. **AI workspace:** expert Agents, business Agents, and personal Agents.
3. **Enterprise context:** knowledge, data, memory, identity, and permissions.
4. **Agent runtime:** workflows, evaluation, model access, and API/MCP integrations.
5. **Enterprise systems:** ERP, CRM, OA/DMS, MES, and other business applications.

Governance spans every layer, covering permissions, auditability, security, cost, effectiveness, and business value.
